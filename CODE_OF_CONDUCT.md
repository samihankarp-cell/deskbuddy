# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, welcome people of all backgrounds and experience levels, give and accept feedback kindly, and do not harass, insult or demean anyone. Harassment, sharing other people's private information, and other conduct that is unwelcome in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers through GitHub: open a [private advisory](https://github.com/samihankarp-cell/deskbuddy/security/advisories/new), or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions, and may ban people who break these rules, following the enforcement guidelines in the Covenant.
